Roadmap to the platinum

Fifty-five trophies, one order. The phases below are built from the official descriptions: story first, then the side strands, then activities, then collectibles, then the money cleanup. The platinum pops when the other 54 are done.

Missable trophies

“Missable” here means one thing only: the trophy belongs to a single story mission or chapter, so it can only pop while that moment is live. Nothing in this list is permanently missable if mission replay exists — confirm in game whether a mission can be replayed — but do these when they come up rather than leaving them for later. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story.
